THE RULES OF STEEL

Controls & combat

On a keyboard

W A S DMove freely
SPACEStrike in your facing direction
SHIFTHold to block the front arc
EDodge in your move direction

On touch

Use the left thumbstick to move. Strike, Block, and Dodge sit under your right thumb. Buttons support multi-touch.

The exchange

Blocking turns away attacks from the direction you face. A dodge grants a brief moment of invulnerability. Watch the cooldowns beneath your health bar.

No stamina, no timers, no running away. First warrior to fall loses.
